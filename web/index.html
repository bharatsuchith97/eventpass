Features

Everything you need to run the door

One place for your guest list, invitations, passes and check-in, so your team is not juggling spreadsheets on the day.

Guest lists that stay tidy

Add guests one by one or import a CSV. You see a preview with every problem row flagged before anything is saved. Keep your own guest IDs and notes, and tag VIPs, speakers and sponsors.

A personal QR pass for everyone

Every guest gets their own pass to open on their phone, print, or download as a PDF. If a pass is lost or shared, issue a new one and the old QR stops working.

Invitations and RSVP

Email invitations with the pass included. Guests confirm or decline from their pass page, and you can see who has opened their invitation.

Check-in in seconds

Scan with the phone camera or search by name or guest ID. A full-screen green, amber or red result tells staff exactly what to do. Each pass works only once.

Live dashboard and reports

Watch arrivals as they happen, then download attendance, no-show, RSVP and complete guest reports as spreadsheets once the doors close.

The right access for each person

Admins, event managers and check-in staff each see only what they need. Door staff never see full guest email addresses.

Privacy

Your guests' details stay yours

Guest lists hold names, emails and phone numbers. Inviteley is built so that information stays with the company it belongs to.

  • Private to your company. Every event, guest and pass belongs to your company account, and other companies can never see or change them.
  • QR codes carry no personal details. A pass holds only a random code, so a photo of it reveals nothing about the guest.
  • Only your team gets in. Every action is checked against the person's role, and important changes are recorded in an audit log.
  • Hard-to-fake passes. Cancelled, expired, already-used and wrong-event passes are all turned away at the door.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do my guests need to install an app?

No. Each guest gets a link to their pass, which opens in any browser. Passes can also be printed or saved as a PDF.

Can a pass be used twice?

No. A pass checks in exactly once, even if two staff scan it at the same moment. A repeat scan shows when the guest was first checked in.

Can I import my existing guest list?

Yes. Upload a CSV file and Inviteley previews it and points out any rows with problems before anything is saved. Excel sheets can be saved as CSV first.

Who can see my guest data?

Only your team. Everything in Inviteley is tied to your company account, other companies can never see it, and QR codes contain a random code rather than names or email addresses.

Does check-in work on a phone?

Yes. Staff scan QR codes with the phone camera in the browser, or search for a guest by name if a pass is not at hand.

How do I get started?

Request a company account. Once it has been approved you get an email and can sign in and create your first event.
